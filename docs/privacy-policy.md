# Animal Almanac privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Animal Almanac is an educational app about animals living and extinct. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking, and the developer does not collect, store or sell any of your information.

## What stays on your phone

Everything you do in the app is saved only on your own device:

- your profile name, profile picture and chosen title
- species you have found, quiz results, check-ins and other progress
- photos you choose to add to species pages

The developer never receives any of this. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

## Location

When you tap **Check in**, the app asks for your location once to see whether you are at a museum, zoo or fossil site. Your location is compared with the list of places built into the app, on your phone. It is not sent anywhere or saved. The app never uses your location in the background.

## Photos

If you choose a photo for a species page or as your profile picture, the app keeps a copy on your phone. It only sees the photos you pick.

## Multiplayer games (Head to Head, Peer Review and Bone War)

When you play Head to Head, Peer Review or Bone War with other people, your phone connects directly to theirs. To do this:

- Your profile name, title and profile picture are sent to the other players in Head to Head and Peer Review. In Bone War, your name, the cards in your deck, your card and theme colours, and small copies of any photos you have added for the animals in your deck are sent to the other player, so they see your cards as you do. They are only shown during the game and are not kept.
- A public connection service (PeerJS, 0.peerjs.com) and Google's and Cloudflare's connection helpers (STUN servers) are used to help the phones find each other. Like any internet service, they can see your device's internet (IP) address while connecting. The game normally goes directly between the players' phones. When the phones' networks don't allow a direct link (common on mobile data), the game's messages are passed through a relay (TURN) server; they are encrypted, so the relay cannot read them, and it keeps nothing.

Nothing is sent unless you start or join a game.

## Pictures and articles

Species pictures are loaded from Wikimedia Commons (upload.wikimedia.org) when you view them, with credit to their authors. As with any website, Wikimedia can see your device's internet (IP) address when a picture loads; the app sends nothing else to them. See the Wikimedia privacy policy: https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
